# Word Journey v2.19.0 — Themed Level Packs

## What's New

### 🎁 Themed Level Packs — Now Fully Playable!
All six seasonal themed level packs are now live with **100 hand-curated levels each**:

| Pack | Season | Theme |
|------|--------|-------|
| 🐣 Easter & Spring | Mar 15 – Apr 20 | Spring renewal, birds, nature |
| 💕 Valentine's Day | Feb 1 – 14 | Love, romance, celebration |
| ☀️ Summer | Jun 1 – Aug 31 | Beach, ocean, sunshine, BBQ |
| 🎃 Halloween | Oct 1 – 31 | Spooky, horror, supernatural |
| 🦃 Thanksgiving | Nov 1 – 28 | Harvest, gratitude, feasting |
| 🎄 Christmas & Winter | Dec 1 – 31 | Holiday, snow, winter magic |

**600 total themed words** — all exactly 5 letters, unique per pack.

### 🗺️ Browse All Packs Screen
- New **Themed Packs** screen accessible from the home page
- Shows all 6 packs with your progress (levels completed / 100)
- Colour-coded progress bars per season
- Available to play year-round — not just during the season
- Active season badge displayed for the currently active pack

### 🏠 Home Screen Updates
- Active season card now shows a real **Play Now** button (instead of "coming soon")
- **Browse All Themed Packs** button always visible in the Theme Level Packs section

### 🔧 Technical Improvements
- Progress tracked independently for each seasonal pack (survives app restarts)
- In-progress game state saved per seasonal pack (can quit and resume)
- No impact on regular/easy/hard/VIP level progress
- 19 new unit tests verifying word integrity (5 letters, no duplicates, 100 words/pack)
